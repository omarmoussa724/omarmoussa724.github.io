About

Background

I studied law at Cairo University, then spent seven years on the front line of operations — answering calls at Teleperformance, running the floor at Etisal, reporting straight to the C-suite as a support supervisor at SYMPL, and finally sitting between engineering and merchants as a UAT specialist at Fawry, Egypt's largest payments network.

That path taught me what a report is actually for: someone downstream needs to make a call, fast, without re-asking the analyst three follow-up questions. So when I moved into data full-time at Smart & Value Group, I brought that instinct with me — a 6-page Power BI dashboard now tracks 3,300+ investment contracts and 724M EGP in AUM for the executive team, flags cash-flow risk on 41M EGP in monthly obligations, and feeds the CS and Finance teams the same numbers so nobody argues about whose spreadsheet is right.

Outside client work, I write structured knowledge bases on Tableau and Power BI — partly to teach, mostly because writing the steps down is how I actually learn them.
